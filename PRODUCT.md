# PRODUCT.md — Vitor Igor dos Santos | Portfolio

## Product Lane

**Brand** — This is a personal marketing surface. A single-page portfolio designed to convert visitors (recruiters, hiring managers) into contacts. It is not a dashboard or application UI; every design decision should serve impression, credibility, and clarity of identity.

---

## Audience & Personas

### Primary: Brazilian Recruiters & Hiring Managers
- Mid-to-senior talent acquisition professionals at Brazilian tech companies and software houses.
- Scan portfolios quickly; prioritize legibility, professionalism, and proof of skill.
- Expect Portuguese as the default language.

### Secondary: International Recruiters & Hiring Managers
- English-speaking recruiters scouting for remote-first talent.
- Value clean, modern presentation over flashiness.
- Triggered by the language toggle (PT → EN).

### Common expectations across both:
- Fast load time (static site, no frameworks).
- Clear identity: who is this person, what do they do, how to contact them.
- Confidence without arrogance.

---

## Brand Voice & Principles

**Tone:** Ambitious & sharp — a developer who means business.

| Principle | Description |
|---|---|
| **Precision** | Every word and pixel earns its place. No filler copy, no decorative noise. |
| **Confidence** | The portfolio speaks for itself. No over-explanation or hedging language. |
| **Craft** | Code-first aesthetic. Monospace accents (`Fira Code`), binary matrix animation, and structured layouts signal that the owner thinks like a developer. |
| **Clarity** | Any recruiter, regardless of technical background, must immediately understand: who Vitor is, what he does, and how to reach him. |

## Anti-References (Hard Stops)

These patterns are **explicitly forbidden** in any AI-generated suggestions for this project:

- ❌ **Purple-to-blue gradients** — generic AI slop; the brand uses pure `#146CFF` blue, not gradient washes.
- ❌ **Generic stock illustrations or avatars** — only real profile photo (`assets/profile.png`) is used.
- ❌ **Pastel or soft color palettes** — the palette is dark, confident, and high-contrast.
- ❌ **Decorative card nesting** — no "card inside a card inside a section" layouts.
- ❌ **Emoji-heavy copy** — the portfolio copy is sharp and professional; decorative emoji in body text undermine credibility.
- ❌ **Inter font** — the project uses Poppins + Fira Code; do not swap or introduce Inter.
- ❌ **Glassy frosted-glass overload** — backdrop-filter is used sparingly (navbar only); do not apply it to content cards.

---

## Success Criteria

A successful visitor journey looks like:
1. Landing on the Hero: immediately understands "This is Vitor, a Full Stack Developer."
2. Scrolling to About: understands his stack, studies, and attitude.
3. Reaching Contacts: clicks WhatsApp or GitHub without friction.

The portfolio succeeds if it feels **professional, fast, and memorable** — not loud, not generic.
